Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

App name: Electricity Token kWh Calc (Indonesian name: Token Listrik: Berapa kWh?) (com.htd.tokenlistrik)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Token kWh” (“Token Listrik”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app estimates how many kWh you get from a prepaid electricity token in Indonesia, calculates the token amount needed for a number of kWh, estimates the electricity cost of household appliances, and shows tariff and meter information. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the amounts, tariffs, or appliance details you enter. All calculations happen 100% locally on your device. The app never asks for your meter number, customer ID, token number, name, contacts, or location. It has no accounts, does not use the camera, does not sell tokens, and does not connect to any PLN system.

The app saves a few settings only on your device: that you accepted the first-launch notice, your chosen power, tariff, and PPJ percentage, and the appliances you added. You can clear them by clearing the app data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your calculations or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Calculating kWh and costs does not require any permissions. The app does not use the camera.

7. About the Results

Results are estimates using the common formula (amount − PPJ) ÷ tariff. Tariffs follow public announcements and can change every 3 months, PPJ differs by region, and meter codes come from news articles. The numbers on your receipt or meter can differ.

Token kWh is an independent app and is NOT an official app of PT PLN (Persero). It is not affiliated with PLN, the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, any local government, or any meter brand.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
