Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

App name: Text Case Converter: UPPERCASE (Indonesian name: Ubah Huruf Kapital & Judul EYD) (com.htd.hurufkapital)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Case Converter” (“Huruf Kapital”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app changes letter case (UPPERCASE, lowercase, Sentence case, Capitalize Each Word, Title Case, Indonesian EYD title rules, and person names) in text you type, paste, share, or select in other apps. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit your text. Text you type, paste, share to the app, or select in other apps through the text selection menu is converted 100% locally on your device and is never uploaded by this app. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

The app saves on your device only a few settings (your chosen style and options). The text in the editor is not saved after you close the app. When you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device clipboard.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your text or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste. The text selection menu (“Change Case”) receives only the text you select, and no ads are shown there. The app does not request access to your files, camera, contacts, or location.

7. About the Results

The styles follow common rules (including the general EYD V title rules), but language has exceptions. Names, abbreviations, and special terms may need manual correction, so always check important text before using it.

Text Case Converter is an independent app and is not affiliated with Badan Bahasa, the Ministry of Education, Microsoft, Google, WhatsApp, or any other organization.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
