Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

App name: Aesthetic Emoji: Bio & Symbols (Indonesian name: Nama & Emoji Aesthetic: Bio IG) (com.htd.emojiaesthetic)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Aesthetic Emoji” (“Emoji Aesthetic”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app offers aesthetic emoji combos, short bio ideas, aesthetic name ideas and decorations, and symbols that you can copy and share. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the name you type, your favorites, or your copy history. Bios and name decorations are created 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as contacts or location.

The only things the app saves on your device are your favorites, your copy history (the last 100 copied items, which you can delete at any time from the Saved tab), and a few small settings.

The copy buttons only place text on your device's clipboard. The share action opens Android's share sheet; you choose where the text goes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the name you type, your favorites, copy history, or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Typing, copying, favorites, and history do not require any permissions.

7. About the Content

The emoji combos, bios, name ideas, and decorations were put together by us from standard Unicode characters. How they look depends on your device and on the app where you paste them. Please follow the rules of each app where you use a name or bio, and do not use them to impersonate others or to mislead people.

Aesthetic Emoji is an independent app and is not affiliated with Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Meta, or any other company. These names (including the abbreviations IG and WA) are only used to describe where the text can be pasted.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
