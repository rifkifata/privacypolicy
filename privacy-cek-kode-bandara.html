Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

App name: Cek Kode Bandara / Airport Code Finder (com.htd.cekkodebandara)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Cek Kode Bandara” (English name “Airport Code Finder”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app lets you look up airport codes (IATA and ICAO), find airports by city or name, browse airports by country and province, and calculate the straight-line distance between two airports.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the codes or names you search for. Every search and distance calculation runs locally on your device using the reference database that ships with the app, and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no search history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use GPS, the camera or the microphone.

The only things the app saves on your device are a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch, plus a local copy of the reference database that ships with the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your searches or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the purpose of loading Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). The airport features do not require any permissions and work without an internet connection.

7. Data Sources & Accuracy

Information was compiled from public open data. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • Airports, codes, locations, elevations and runways: OurAirports open data (released to the public domain)
  • Part of the time zone data: mwgg/Airports (Copyright (c) 2014 mwgg, MIT License). Time zones of airports not covered there are estimated from nearby airports in the same province or country.
  • Country and region codes: ISO 3166; Indonesian province names follow the 38 provinces of Indonesia

Airport information can change and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Distances are straight-line (great-circle) distances and flight times are rough estimates, not flight schedules. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. Listing a source here is not an endorsement and does not mean that the source is affiliated with this app. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
