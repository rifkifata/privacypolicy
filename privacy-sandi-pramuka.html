Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

App name: Morse Code & Scout Ciphers (Indonesian name: Sandi Pramuka: Morse Semaphore) (com.htd.sandipramuka)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Morse & Cipher” (“Sandi Pramuka”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app converts messages to and from Morse code, flag semaphore, and other classic scout ciphers, shows cipher tables, and can play Morse code as sound, flashlight blinks, or vibration. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the messages you type. All calculations run 100% locally on your device and are never uploaded by this app. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

The app saves on your device only a few settings (your chosen cipher, rules, and playback options). Messages are not saved after you close the app. When you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device clipboard. When you tap Share image, a temporary picture of the code is created in the app's private cache and passed only to the app you choose.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your messages or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). The app also uses VIBRATE to play Morse code as vibration when you choose it, and turns the camera flash on and off as a flashlight when you choose it (without camera permission; no photos or video are taken). The app does not request access to your files, camera images, contacts, or location.

7. About the Results

Morse code and flag semaphore follow the international standards. Other scout ciphers have no single official form; the rule used is always shown in the app. If your group uses a different rule, follow your leader's key.

Morse Code & Scout Ciphers is an independent app and is not affiliated with Gerakan Pramuka, Kwartir Nasional, any scouting organization, Google, or any other organization.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
