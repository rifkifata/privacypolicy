Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

App name: Javanese Script: Hanacaraka (Indonesian name: Translate Aksara Jawa & Latin) (com.htd.aksarajawa)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Javanese Script” (“Aksara Jawa”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app converts Latin text to Javanese script (aksara Jawa) and back, provides an on-screen Javanese keyboard, and shows tables of Javanese letters and signs. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the text you type or convert. All conversions happen 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. It does not use the camera and does not keep a history of what you convert. When you tap Share image, the image is created on your device in a temporary cache folder and is shared only with the app you choose.

The app saves a few settings only on your device: that you accepted the first-launch notice, your space and murda options, and the text size.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your text or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Converting text does not require any permissions. Sharing an image uses a temporary file in the app cache, so no storage permission is needed. The app does not use the camera.

7. About the Results

The app follows the Pedoman Penulisan Aksara Jawa (2002) and the Unicode standard. Javanese spelling varies between regions and schools, so results can differ from what a teacher or book uses. Please check important text with a teacher or an expert. Javanese letters are shown with the Noto Sans Javanese font (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Javanese Script is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government office, school, language institute, or publisher.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
