Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

App name: Cek IMEI Offline / IMEI Checker Offline (com.htd.cekimeioffline)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Cek IMEI Offline” (English title “IMEI Checker Offline”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app explains the structure of a mobile phone IMEI number: whether it matches the check digit formula, the Type Allocation Code (TAC) with an estimated phone brand and model, the TAC reporting body and the serial number.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the numbers you enter. When you type an IMEI, it is decoded locally on your device and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no search history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use the camera.

The app does not read your phone's IMEI or any other device identifier. The “Show this phone's IMEI” button only opens the About phone screen in your device settings; the IMEI is shown there by your phone's own system and is not passed to this app.

The app does not connect to any government, operator or GSMA database (including the Indonesian IMEI registration system or the Customs IMEI check) and does not check whether an IMEI is registered, blocked or reported lost.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads, including banner, interstitial and app open ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app contains links that open official websites (such as the Indonesian Customs IMEI check at beacukai.go.id) in your web browser. The app does not send any number to these websites. Anything you do on them is governed by the privacy policies of their operators.

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the numbers you enter or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the purpose of loading Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). The app does not request phone permissions (such as READ_PHONE_STATE). Decoding numbers does not require any permissions and works without an internet connection.

7. Information Sources & Accuracy

The meaning of the digits and the phone data are based on the following publicly available information. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • IMEI structure and check digit formula: 3GPP TS 23.003 and GSMA TS.06 “IMEI Allocation and Approval Process” (gsma.com)
  • List of TAC reporting bodies: GSMA TS.06, Annex A
  • Phone brand and model data were taken from the internet: a public TAC database compiled from various online sources (github.com/MoazEb/tac-database). This data is not official GSMA data and may be incomplete or wrong.

A correctly structured IMEI is not necessarily registered, genuine or unblocked, and the brand and model shown are estimates only. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. The records of the authorities and manufacturers always prevail. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
