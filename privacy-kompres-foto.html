Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

App name: Photo Compressor: JPG to 200KB (Indonesian name: Kompres Foto JPG: 100KB 200KB) (com.htd.kompresfoto)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Photo Compressor” (“Kompres Foto”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app reduces (or increases) the file size of photos to a size you choose, converts JPG, PNG, WEBP, and HEIC photos, and can combine photos into a PDF. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit your photos. All processing happens 100% locally on your device, and photos are never uploaded by this app. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. Location data is removed from the compressed photos. Original photos are never changed or deleted.

The app saves on your device only the results you create (in your gallery folder “Kompres Foto”, or Download/Kompres Foto for PDF), temporary copies used for sharing, and a few settings (your chosen size, format, and options).

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your photos or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Photos are chosen with the Android photo picker or shared to the app by you, so no permission to read all your photos is needed. On Android 7–9 only, the app asks for storage permission (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) to save results to your gallery. The app does not use the camera.

7. About the Results

Compressing lowers image quality and, if needed, the dimensions, so some detail can be lost. Always check that the result meets the requirements of the form or website you upload to.

Photo Compressor is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government agency, school, registration portal, or other app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
