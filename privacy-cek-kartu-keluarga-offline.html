Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

App name: Cek Kartu Keluarga Offline / KK Family Card Checker Offline (com.htd.cekkartukeluarga)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Cek Kartu Keluarga Offline” (English title “KK Family Card Checker Offline”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app explains the structure of the 16-digit Indonesian family card number (nomor Kartu Keluarga, KK): the area where the card was issued, the date the number was created and its serial number.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the numbers you enter. When you type a number, it is decoded locally on your device and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no search history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use the camera.

The app does not connect to any government database (including the population database of Dukcapil or the IKD app) and does not look up the owner, family members, address or status of any number.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads, including banner, interstitial and app open ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app contains links that open official government websites (such as dukcapil.kemendagri.go.id) in your web browser. The app does not send any number to these websites. Anything you do on them is governed by the privacy policies of their operators.

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the numbers you enter or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the purpose of loading Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Decoding numbers does not require any permissions and works without an internet connection.

7. Information Sources & Accuracy

The meaning of the digits and the area names are based on the following publicly available information. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • Meaning of the 16 digits of the family card number: explanation published by the Population and Civil Registration Office of Klaten Regency (dukcapil.klaten.go.id)
  • Province, regency/city and district codes, including older codes used before regions were split: Indonesian Ministry of Home Affairs administrative area data, as compiled in the open dataset cahyadsn/wilayah

A correctly structured number is not necessarily a registered or valid number, and a family card number can change, for example after moving house. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. The records of Dukcapil always prevail. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
