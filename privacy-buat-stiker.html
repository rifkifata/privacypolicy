Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

App name: Sticker Maker: Make Stickers (Indonesian name: Stiker WA: Buat Stiker Lucu) (com.htd.buatstiker)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Sticker Maker” (“Buat Stiker”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app lets you create stickers from your photos or your own text, save them into sticker packs, add those packs to WhatsApp, and share stickers with other apps.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, upload, or store your photos, the text you type, or your stickers on any server. Stickers are created 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

Photos are chosen with Android's built-in photo picker, so the app only gets access to the photos you pick, and no gallery permission is needed. The stickers you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, together with the pack names you choose. Deleting a pack or uninstalling the app removes them.

3. Sharing with WhatsApp and Other Apps

When you tap “Add to WhatsApp”, WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) on your device reads that sticker pack from the app, as described in WhatsApp's official sticker integration. When you tap “Share”, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where the sticker goes. We do not send your stickers anywhere ourselves.

4. Third-Party Services

Google ML Kit (background removal). Background removal runs on your device using ML Kit Subject Segmentation, provided through Google Play services. Your photos are processed on the device and are not sent to us. Google Play services may download the model and, according to Google, ML Kit may collect device and app information, per-installation identifiers, and performance and diagnostic data to maintain and improve the service. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Google AdMob (ads). Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or upload your photos, text, stickers, or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Data handled by Google services follows Google's policies.

7. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads and to let Google Play services download the background removal model. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Picking photos, creating stickers, and saving packs do not require any permissions.

8. Your Responsibility

Only use photos you own or have permission to use. Do not create stickers that harass, insult, or impersonate other people, or that contain hateful, violent, or adult content. Follow the rules of WhatsApp and any app where you share stickers.

Sticker Maker is an independent app and is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta, or any other company. WhatsApp (including the abbreviation WA) is a trademark of its owner and is mentioned only because sticker packs can be added to it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
