Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

App name: Roman Numeral Converter & Date (Indonesian name: Angka Romawi & Bulan Romawi) (com.htd.angkaromawi)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Roman Numerals” (“Angka Romawi”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app converts numbers to Roman numerals and back, converts dates to Roman numerals, and shows Roman numeral charts. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the numbers, Roman numerals, or dates you enter. All conversions happen 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. It does not keep a history of what you convert.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that remembers you accepted the first-launch notice.

The copy buttons only place the result on your device's clipboard.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your numbers, dates, or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Converting and copying do not require any permissions.

7. About the Results

Results use the standard modern form of Roman numerals and the overline (vinculum) convention for numbers from 4,000 up to 3,999,999. Some books, clocks, and inscriptions use other styles. Please double-check important results, such as those used for tattoos, engravings, or official documents.

Roman Numerals is an independent app and is not affiliated with any school, government agency, publisher, or company.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
