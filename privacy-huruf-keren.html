Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

App name: Fancy Text: Cool Font & Nick (Indonesian name: Huruf Keren: Font Aesthetic) (com.htd.hurufkeren)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Fancy Text” (“Huruf Keren”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app turns the text you type into fancy letter styles made from Unicode characters, adds decorations for nicknames, provides special spaces and blank text, and offers a collection of symbols that you can copy and share. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the text you type, the text you copy, your favorites, or your copy history. All styles are created 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

The only things the app saves on your device are your favorite styles and decorations, your copy history (the last 100 copied items, which you can delete at any time from the History tab), and a few small settings.

The copy buttons only place text on your device's clipboard. The share action opens Android's share sheet; you choose where the text goes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your text, favorites, copy history, or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Typing, styling, copying, favorites, and history do not require any permissions.

7. About the Styles

The letter styles are made from standard Unicode characters, not real fonts. How they look depends on the device and on the app where the text is pasted, and some apps or games may not accept certain characters. Please follow the rules of each app or game where you use your text, and do not use styled text to impersonate others or to mislead people.

Fancy Text is an independent app and is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Free Fire, Mobile Legends, or any other app, game, or company. These names are only used to describe where the copied text can be pasted.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
