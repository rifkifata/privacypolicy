Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

App name: Land Area Units: m², Hectare (Indonesian name: Tumbak ke Meter: Bata, Ru, Bau) (com.htd.satuantanah)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Land Units” (“Satuan Tanah”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app converts land area units (m², tumbak, bata, ru, ubin, bau, are, hectare), calculates area from length × width, and calculates land prices from the price you type. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the areas or prices you type. All calculations run 100% locally on your device and are never uploaded by this app. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

The app saves on your device only a few settings (your chosen units and any unit values you change). The numbers you type are not saved after you close the app. When you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device clipboard.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your numbers or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). The app does not request access to your files, camera, contacts, or location.

7. About the Results

Traditional land units differ by region and source. The app uses common values that you can change. Land certificates and official documents use square meters (m²); for legal matters and transactions, check with your village office, land office, or land deed official (PPAT). The app does not show market prices.

Land Area Units is an independent app and is not affiliated with BPN / the Ministry of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning, any land office, notary, Google, or any other organization.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
