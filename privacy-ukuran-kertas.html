Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

App name: Paper Size Chart: A4 & Letter (Indonesian name: Ukuran Kertas: A4, F4 & Pixel) (com.htd.ukurankertas)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Paper Size” (“Ukuran Kertas”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app shows paper sizes (A, B, US, photo, and envelope sizes) in centimeters, millimeters, inches, and pixels, compares two paper sizes, and finds the closest paper size for a width and height you type. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the paper sizes you select or the numbers you type. All calculations happen 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. It does not use the camera and does not keep a history of what you look up.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that remembers you accepted the first-launch notice.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your sizes or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Showing and converting paper sizes does not require any permissions. The app does not use the camera.

7. About the Results

A, B, and envelope sizes follow ISO 216 and ISO 269; Letter, Legal, and Tabloid follow US standards. F4/Folio, Kuarto, and some envelope and photo sizes have no official standard, so the app shows the most common size; real paper can differ by a few millimeters. Please check your paper and printer settings before printing.

Paper Size Chart is an independent app and is not affiliated with ISO, any standards body, any paper or printer brand, Microsoft, or Canva.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
