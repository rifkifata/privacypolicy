Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

App name: Kalender Bali & Otonan (com.htd.kalenderbaliotonan)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Kalender Bali & Otonan”. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app lets you view a Balinese Saka and pawukon calendar with Hindu Balinese holy days, full and new moon dates, calculate otonan (Balinese birthday) dates and birth characteristics, and view the Javanese date of any day.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the birth dates or any other dates you enter. Every calculation runs locally on your device and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use the camera, the microphone or location.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch.

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the dates you enter or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

7. Content Sources & Accuracy

The calendar follows traditional Balinese and Javanese rules. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • Pawukon, wewaran, sasih, purnama and tilem, and Saka year calculations use the BalineseDate library by Ida Bagus Putu Peradnya Dinata (Apache License 2.0), based on I.B.S. Ardhana, Pokok-Pokok Wariga (2005). The holy day list was ported from balinese-date-js-lib (Apache License 2.0) and extended by the developer.
  • Character descriptions were rewritten by the developer with reference to babadbali.com. Javanese dates follow Serat Pawukon (Padmasusastra, 1903) and Kabudayan Paugeraning Taun Jawa (R. Tanaya, 1971).
  • National holidays and collective leave days follow the joint ministerial decree (SKB 3 Menteri) of each year. Hijri dates follow the calendar of the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs (Kemenag).

Dates of purnama, tilem and some holy days may differ by one day from printed calendars in certain years. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. Listing a source here is not an endorsement and does not mean that the source is affiliated with this app. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
