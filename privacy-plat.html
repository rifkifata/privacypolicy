1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app "Cek Plat Kendaraan Offline". We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app is designed to help users look up regional information based on Indonesian vehicle license plate numbers offline.

2. Important Disclaimer: Not a Government Entity

This app DOES NOT represent, and is NOT affiliated with or endorsed by, any government entity, including the Indonesian National Police (Polri), Samsat, or any other state institution.

Information Source: The vehicle license plate region codes (TNKB) provided in this app are predictions based on general public information and regulatory standards from the Indonesian Traffic Police Corps (Korlantas Polri). The official sources of this information can be accessed via the official government websites: https://korlantas.polri.go.id or https://polri.go.id. The information in this app should not be used for legal or official administrative purposes.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as name, email, phone number, location, or device ID. The core functionality of checking license plates works 100% offline without sending data to any server.

4. Third-Party Services (Ads)

While the core features are offline, our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect certain non-personal data (such as device type, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve relevant ads and improve performance. To ensure compliance with policies, ads served in this app are configured to be suitable for general audiences.

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no user data stored on our servers. Ad-related data is handled securely by Google and its partners according to their privacy policies.

7. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet solely for the purpose of loading ads via Google AdMob. No other sensitive permissions (such as camera, location, or contacts) are requested or used.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
