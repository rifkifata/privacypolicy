Privacy Policy

Last updated: 03 September 2026

App name: Kode Pos Offline

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Kode Pos Offline”. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app is designed to help users search for Indonesian postal codes (Kode Pos) safely, quickly, and entirely offline.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit your search queries or location data. The app relies on a pre-populated local database containing public regional data. When you search for an area or postal code, the process happens 100% locally on your device. We do not have servers, and we do not collect any personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

While the core postal code search feature works entirely offline, our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection and may collect certain non-personal data (such as device type, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve relevant ads and improve performance.

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data or search histories, there is no personal data stored on any external servers to be breached. Ad-related data is handled securely by Google according to their policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) solely for the purpose of loading Google AdMob ads. The core postal code directory functionality does not require any special permissions (like GPS or Location).

7. Disclaimer & Affiliation

This application is an independent utility tool and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to PT Pos Indonesia or any Indonesian Government entity. The app uses publicly available regional and postal code data and does not access any official government or corporate databases in real-time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
