Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

App name: Cek No Rangka & Mesin Offline / Check VIN & Engine Number (com.htd.ceknomorrangka)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Cek No Rangka & Mesin Offline” (English title “Check VIN & Engine Number”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app explains the meaning of a vehicle frame number (VIN, called nomor rangka in Indonesia) and engine number (nomor mesin) for motorcycles, cars and trucks: the manufacturer, country of assembly, estimated model, model year, check digit and other parts of the number.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the frame numbers or engine numbers you enter. When you type a number, it is decoded locally on your device using the reference database that ships with the app, and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no search history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use the camera.

The app does not connect to any government, police, Samsat or manufacturer database, and it does not look up the owner, registration, tax or legal status of any vehicle.

The only things the app saves on your device are a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch, plus a local copy of the reference database that ships with the app.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the numbers you enter or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

The frame and engine number features do not require any permissions and work without an internet connection.

7. Data Sources & Accuracy

Information was compiled from public sources. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • VIN structure, country codes and check digit formula: ISO 3779 / ISO 3780 as described in Wikipedia “Vehicle identification number” (CC BY-SA); Indonesian definition of the vehicle identification number (NIK/VIN): Minister of Industry Regulation No. 34 of 2019 and SNI 09-1411
  • Manufacturer codes (WMI): U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), vPIC; Wikipedia and Wikibooks (CC BY-SA)
  • Honda motorcycle frame and engine type codes: a public parts catalogue of an authorized Honda dealer
  • Other model and engine code patterns: compiled statistically from publicly published Indonesian government auction and vehicle inventory documents. Individual vehicle numbers from these documents are not included in the app.

Manufacturers use their own internal codes for the vehicle type, plant and engine, and most of them do not publish these codes. Model, model year and engine information is therefore an estimate and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Not every manufacturer uses the check digit or the model year code. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. Listing a source here is not an endorsement and does not mean that the source is affiliated with this app. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
