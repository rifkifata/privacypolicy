Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

App name: Emoticon BB Kaomoji

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Emoticon BB Kaomoji”. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app is designed to provide users with a wide collection of text-based emoticons and kaomojis that can be easily copied and shared.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit your personal data. While our app connects to our server via an API to fetch the latest emoticons (using GET requests), we do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as names, email addresses, or location. The server connection is strictly used for delivering the emoticon content to your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection and may collect certain non-personal data (such as device type, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve relevant ads and improve performance.

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no user data stored on our external servers to be breached. Although the app communicates with our server to download emoticon content, it does not upload or track your personal information. The process of copying emoticons to your clipboard happens 100% locally on your device. Ad-related data is handled securely by Google according to their policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for two purposes: fetching the latest emoticon content from our API server and loading Google AdMob ads. It also utilizes standard device features like the clipboard to allow you to copy and paste emoticons, which does not require special privacy permissions.

7. Disclaimer

This application is an independent utility tool designed for entertainment and communication purposes. The kaomojis and emoticons provided within the app are derived from common public domain text combinations.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
