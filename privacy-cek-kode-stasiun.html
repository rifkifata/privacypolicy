Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

App name: Cek Kode Stasiun / Train Station Code Finder (com.htd.cekkodestasiun)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Cek Kode Stasiun” (English name “Train Station Code Finder”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app lets you look up Indonesian train station codes, find stations by name or city, browse stations by province, operational area or service, and see the stations nearest to you.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the codes or names you search for. Every search runs locally on your device using the reference database that ships with the app, and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no search history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts or photos. The app does not use the camera or the microphone.

The only things the app saves on your device are a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch, plus a local copy of the reference database that ships with the app.

3. Location (Nearest Stations)

The optional “Nearest stations” feature uses your device's location. Location permission is requested only when you tap the button, and you can choose approximate or precise location, or refuse. When you use the feature, your location is read once, used only on your device to calculate the straight-line distance to stations and sort them, and then discarded. Your location is never saved, never sent to us or to any server by this app, and never shared with third parties by this app. The app does not track your location in the background. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your Android settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device and app information, and ad interaction data. The app does not pass your GPS location to AdMob. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your searches, your location or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

7. Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: only for loading Google AdMob ads. Searching works without an internet connection.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: only for the optional “Nearest stations” feature, requested when you tap its button, and processed only on your device.
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID).

8. Data Sources & Accuracy

Information was compiled from public open data. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • Station list, codes, provinces, operational areas, status and elevations: adapted from the Indonesian Wikipedia article “Daftar stasiun kereta api di Indonesia” and related station articles (Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0). The station dataset bundled with the app is shared under the same license.
  • Coordinates and regencies/cities: Wikidata (CC0)

Station information can change and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Distances to the nearest stations are straight-line distances, not travel distances. The app does not contain train schedules, routes, fares or tickets. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. Listing a source here is not an endorsement and does not mean that the source is affiliated with this app. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
