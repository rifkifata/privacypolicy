Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

App name: Kalender Jawa & Weton Hari Ini (com.htd.kalenderjawawetonhariini)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Kalender Jawa & Weton Hari Ini”. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app lets you see today's Javanese weton and pasaran, browse a monthly Javanese calendar with Hijri dates and national holidays, convert dates, find dates by weton, check a birth weton, and add a home screen widget.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the birth dates or any other dates you enter. Every calculation runs locally on your device and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use the camera, the microphone or location.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch. The home screen widget only reads the current date on your device to show today's weton.

Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the dates you enter or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: only for loading Google AdMob ads. All features work without an internet connection.
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID).

7. Content Sources & Accuracy

The calculations follow traditional Javanese rules. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • Calculation rules from Serat Pawukon (Padmasusastra, 1903) and Kabudayan Paugeraning Taun Jawa (R. Tanaya, 1971), digitised by Yayasan Sastra Lestari, and from the Kitab Primbon Betaljemur Adammakna. All interpretation texts in the app were rewritten by the developer.
  • Some descriptions (for example pranata mangsa) are adapted from Indonesian Wikipedia articles such as “Kalender Jawa” and “Pranata mangsa” (Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • National holidays and collective leave days follow the joint ministerial decree (SKB 3 Menteri) of each year. Hijri dates follow the calendar of the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs (Kemenag) and may differ by one day from some organizations.

Javanese primbon has many regional versions, so results may differ from other books or websites. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. Listing a source here is not an endorsement and does not mean that the source is affiliated with this app. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
