Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

App name: LCM & GCF Calculator: Steps (Indonesian name: FPB dan KPK + Pohon Faktor) (com.htd.fpbkpk)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “LCM GCF” (“FPB KPK”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app calculates the greatest common factor (GCF) and least common multiple (LCM) of 2 to 5 whole numbers and shows the steps (factor trees, prime factorization, the table method, multiples and factors). Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the numbers you type. All calculations run 100% locally on your device and are never uploaded by this app. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

The numbers on the screen are not saved after you close the app. When you tap Copy, the answer is placed on your device clipboard. When you tap Share image, a temporary picture of the steps is created in the app's private cache and passed only to the app you choose.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your numbers or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). The app does not request access to your files, camera, contacts, or location.

7. About the Results

The results follow standard arithmetic. Teachers may ask for a specific method or way of writing the steps, so follow your teacher's instructions.

LCM & GCF Calculator is an independent app and is not affiliated with any ministry of education, school, publisher, Google, or any other organization.
