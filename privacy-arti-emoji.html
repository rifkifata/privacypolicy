Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

App name: Emoji Meaning & Heart Meaning (Indonesian name: Arti Emoji & Arti Love Warna) (com.htd.artiemoji)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Emoji Meaning” (“Arti Emoji”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app explains what emoji mean in chats, shows heart color meanings, lets you search emoji by word, and lets you paste a message to see the meaning of each emoji in it. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit what you search, the messages you paste, your favorites, or your copy history. All searching and emoji lookups happen 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location.

The only things the app saves on your device are your favorite emoji, your copy history (the last 100 copied items, which you can delete at any time from the Saved tab), and a few small settings.

The copy buttons only place emoji on your device's clipboard. The share action opens Android's share sheet; you choose where the emoji goes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your searches, pasted messages, favorites, copy history, or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Searching, pasting, copying, favorites, and history do not require any permissions.

7. About the Meanings

Emoji names and official keywords come from the Unicode CLDR project (© Unicode, Inc., used under the Unicode License). The meanings in chats are written by us based on how emoji are commonly used. They are not official, and the same emoji can mean different things to different people and conversations. How emoji look depends on your device and on the app where you use them.

Emoji Meaning is an independent app and is not affiliated with Unicode, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Meta, Apple, Google, Samsung, or any other company. These names are only used to describe where emoji are used.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
