Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

App name: Cek Arti NIP ASN & PPPK / NIP Decoder: ASN & PPPK (com.htd.artinip)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Cek Arti NIP ASN & PPPK” (English title “NIP Decoder: ASN & PPPK”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app explains the meaning of the 18-digit employee number of Indonesian civil servants (NIP PNS) and government contract employees (NI PPPK): the date of birth, appointment date or year, employment contract code, gender and sequence number, together with estimated age, length of service and retirement dates.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the numbers you enter. When you type a number, it is decoded locally on your device and the result is only shown on your screen. The app has no search history, no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect personal data such as names, contacts, photos or location. The app does not use the camera.

The app does not connect to any government database (including BKN, SIASN, MyASN or SSCASN) and does not look up the owner, status or records of any employee number.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that records that you accepted the notice shown at first launch.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

The app contains buttons that open the official BKN websites Mola BKN (monitoring-siasn.bkn.go.id) and MyASN (myasn.bkn.go.id) in your web browser. The app does not send any number to these websites. Anything you do on them is governed by the privacy policies of their operators.

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit the numbers you enter or any other personal data, there is no personal data stored on any external server of ours that could be breached. The data you input never leaves your device through this app. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the purpose of loading Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Decoding numbers does not require any permissions and works without an internet connection.

7. Information Sources & Accuracy

The meaning of the digits and the retirement ages are based on the following publicly available regulations and information. We do not own, operate or control these sources.

  • NIP PNS structure: Regulation of the Head of BKN No. 22 of 2007 on the Civil Servant Identity Number (peraturan.bpk.go.id)
  • NI PPPK structure: BKN Regulation No. 1 of 2019, Article 29 paragraph (5), as amended by BKN Regulation No. 18 of 2020 (peraturan.go.id)
  • Retirement ages: Law No. 20 of 2023 on the State Civil Apparatus, Article 55 (peraturan.bpk.go.id); Government Regulation No. 11 of 2017 as amended by No. 17 of 2020 (peraturan.bpk.go.id); Law No. 14 of 2005 on Teachers and Lecturers; and information published by BKN (bkn.go.id)

Ages, length of service and retirement dates are estimates calculated from the number. The actual retirement age depends on the employee's position and on regulations that may change. A correctly structured number is not necessarily a registered or active number. We do not guarantee that any information is accurate, complete or up to date. Official decrees and the records of BKN and the employing agency always prevail. If you believe any information is wrong, please contact us (see below) and we will review it.
