Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

App name: Shoe Size Converter & Chart (Indonesian name: Ukuran Sepatu: Size US UK EU) (com.htd.ukuransepatu)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Shoe Size” (“Ukuran Sepatu”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app converts shoe sizes between EU, US, UK, foot length (cm), Japanese, and Korean sizes, estimates children's shoe sizes by age, and shows size charts. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the sizes, foot lengths, or ages you select. All conversions happen 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. It does not use the camera and does not keep a history of what you convert.

The only thing the app saves on your device is a small setting that remembers you accepted the first-launch notice.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your sizes or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Converting sizes does not require any permissions. The app does not use the camera.

7. About the Results

There is no single official conversion chart for all shoe brands. Results are general estimates based on foot length and may differ from a brand's chart by about half a size. Children's sizes by age are averages from published research. Please check the brand's or store's size chart before buying.

Shoe Size is an independent app and is not affiliated with any shoe brand, store, marketplace, or standards body.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
