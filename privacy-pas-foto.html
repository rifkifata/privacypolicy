Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

App name: Passport & ID Photo: 3x4 4x6 (Indonesian name: Pas Foto 3x4: Latar Merah Biru) (com.htd.pasfoto)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Passport & ID Photo” (“Pas Foto”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app makes ID photos (such as 2x3, 3x4, 4x6, 35×45 mm, and 2×2 inch) from a photo you take or choose: it replaces the background color, crops the photo around the face, and can arrange several photos on a 4R or A4 print sheet. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit your photos. The person and the face in your photo are detected 100% locally on your device with open source models bundled in the app (MODNet to separate the person from the background and YuNet to find the face, run with LiteRT). Photos are never uploaded by this app and the face is not altered. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. Original photos are never changed or deleted.

The app saves on your device only the results you create (in your gallery folder “Pas Foto”), temporary copies used for sharing or taken with your camera app, and a few settings (your chosen purpose, size, and background color).

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your photos or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Photos are chosen with the Android photo picker, so no permission to read all your photos is needed. The app does not request camera permission: “Take Photo” opens your own camera app, which returns only the photo you take. On Android 7–9 only, the app asks for storage permission (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) to save results to your gallery.

7. About the Results

Photo sizes and background colors in the app are common examples only. Requirements differ between offices and can change, so always check the official requirements before printing or uploading. We are not responsible for rejected photos.

Passport & ID Photo is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government agency (such as Dukcapil, Immigration, Polri, BKN, or the Ministry of Religious Affairs), embassy, school, or company.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
