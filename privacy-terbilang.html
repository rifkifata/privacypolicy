Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

App name: Number to Words Converter (Indonesian name: Terbilang: Angka ke Huruf) (com.htd.terbilang)

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle user information in our app “Number to Words” (“Terbilang”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The app writes numbers and money amounts (rupiah and US dollars) in words in Indonesian and English, including a receipt format and a check style. Everything works offline on your device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit the numbers or amounts you enter. All conversions happen 100% locally on your device. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal data such as names, contacts, or location. It does not keep a history of what you convert.

The only things the app saves on your device are a few small settings: that you accepted the first-launch notice, and your chosen language, currency, letter style, and "and" option.

The copy buttons only place the result on your device's clipboard.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob requires an internet connection. Google may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Whether ads are personalised depends on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Please refer to Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your numbers, amounts, or any other personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers that could be breached. Ad-related data is handled by Google according to its policies.

6. Permissions

This app only requires access to the Internet (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to load Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare advertising-related permissions (for example AD_ID). Converting and copying do not require any permissions.

7. About the Results

Indonesian words follow common Indonesian spelling rules (PUEBI/EYD). English words use the short scale and American style by default, with an option for British "and". Amounts with a currency are rounded to 2 decimal places. Please double-check amounts before using them in receipts, invoices, checks, or other financial or legal documents.

Number to Words is an independent writing tool. It is not a financial app, does not handle money or transactions, and is not affiliated with any bank, government agency, tax office, or company.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
